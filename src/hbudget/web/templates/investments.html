Investments

What the investment and retirement accounts hold, and how that has moved. A summary only: it is not part of the forecast, the debts or the goals, and a change is the difference between two balances, so it includes money paid in or taken out as well as what the market did.

Type the balance from a statement whenever you like; an older date fills in the history. Do not enter account numbers. With the optional bank connection, a linked account can keep its balance up to date by itself (More > Bank connection).

Bring in a file

A statement or a positions file from the broker or the plan (.ofx, .qfx, .csv), or your own table with a date column and a balance column (.csv, .ods, .xlsx). Only dates and totals are read: what the account holds is not kept, and neither is the file. You see what was read before anything is saved.
