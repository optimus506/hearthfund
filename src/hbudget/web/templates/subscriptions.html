Subscriptions

Charges that repeat every month or every year at the same amount, found in the bank and card history. A new price, a new charge and one that stopped are flagged. Bills and loans (bank charges a bill rule matches) are counted apart from the subscriptions. Nothing here moves money or changes the budget.

Stopped

Charges that missed their expected date in the last six months. If one should still be running, check with the company.

Not subscriptions

Payees marked "Not a subscription". They are left out of the totals, also when they are charged again.
