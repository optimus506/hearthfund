Pay, bills and spending

Everything that repeats: the pay that comes in, the bills that go out of checking, and spending budgets such as groceries. Each item makes dated rows in the forecast; changing one updates its future rows, and rows you changed by hand stay as they are. What you owe on cards and loans is on the Debts tab.

Seasonal amounts:

Set the amount by month, e.g. 5-9=120, 10-4=40 (ranges may wrap the year end). Only future rows change.
