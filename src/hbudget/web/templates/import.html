Import a bill system

A YAML or JSON file in the bill-system format (see examples/bill-system.example.yaml), a full backup, or a simple CSV of bills (name, amount, cadence, day). A bill system replaces or merges only the bills; the bank history stays. Restoring a full backup replaces everything in this household, including its bank and card history, uploads and past reconciliations.

Import a spreadsheet

An .ods, .xlsx or .csv checkbook: one row per bill or payday with a name, an amount and a date. You pick the columns in the next step. This replaces the rows of this household.
