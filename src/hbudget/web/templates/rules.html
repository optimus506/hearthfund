Payee rules

Rules link bank descriptions to budget lines. The first rule that matches wins, so order matters. Words are matched anywhere in the description, ignoring case. Alerts and matching tolerances are under Settings.

Starter rules

Ready-made rules for common payees in the United States: paychecks, the usual bills, and everyday spending such as groceries, fuel and streaming. Bill rules are linked to your bills by name, so add your bills first. Only rules you do not have are added, at the end of the list; nothing you have is changed. Afterwards each one is an ordinary rule to edit or delete.
